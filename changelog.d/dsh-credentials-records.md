- **Client publication no longer stops at DeepSeek Harness once the harness has stored a browser session.** Harness builds that keep tagged records write a third `records:` section into `~/.dsh/.credentials.yaml` beside `version` and `refs`, and the router refused any credentials document holding a nested section outside `refs`. Every picker change, provider toggle, catalog refresh, and caller-key rotation therefore failed at the DeepSeek Harness step and left that client's model list frozen at its last good publication. The versioned layout now admits the harness's own `records` section, read with its scope/id keys and JSON payload keys while keeping syntax and duplicate-key checks, and the router never edits it; any other nested section is still refused, and a root-level reference map still may not hold one.
