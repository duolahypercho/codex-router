- **Accept `response.interrupt` control frames on the Responses WebSocket edge.** Codex 0.162 sends `response.interrupt` when the user cancels a turn; the edge rejected it with "Responses WebSocket messages must have type response.create." and never logged the refusal, so a routine cancel surfaced as a client error. Interrupts bypass the serial request queue (queued behind the in-flight turn they would arrive too late), abort the active internal request, and are answered with `response.cancelled`; a turn ended by its own interrupt no longer reports the synthetic 500.
