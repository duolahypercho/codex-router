- **Thinking-model reasoning replay survives fragmented upstream streams.** The read-only Chat Completions tap now checks complete data lines rather than individual HTTP chunks and decodes UTF-8 incrementally, preserving split field names, Unicode reasoning, tool-call identities, and `[DONE]` turn boundaries. Previously fragmentation could silently discard or corrupt the reasoning required by a later tool-result turn. Invalid UTF-8 remains byte-identical on the wire and is not stored for replay.
