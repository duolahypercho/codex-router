- **A failed client publication now says which client was left behind, that later clients were skipped, and why.** When a picker change, provider toggle, or catalog refresh could not republish one client, the error relayed only that publisher's own output: it named no client, did not say publication had stopped there, and the Control Center hid its one explanatory line whenever the cause mentioned a credential, leaving stack frames. The error now opens with `<Client> was not updated, and any client after it was skipped.` on a line of its own, followed by the publisher's report unchanged, so the Control Center always names the client that failed and the terminal shows the cause.
