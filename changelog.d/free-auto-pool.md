- **`free/auto` routes one picker model across the anonymous free pools until all are drained.** `./bin/providers enable free` adds a single Free Auto model. It tries Kilo's `kilo-auto/free`, then Kilo `:free` models, then the OpenCode Zen free lane. It moves on after a 429, quota, 5xx, unavailable or timeout response, but only before text has streamed. A failed member is drained for a cooldown, and the model returns a clear `free_pool_exhausted` 429 when nothing is left. `./bin/control free-pool status|refresh|reset` manages it, and the Control Center Usage page shows the backend that served each request, fallbacks, drains and tokens.
