- **Settings… (⌘,) lands on the Control Center's Settings page, from the macOS tray and from the Control Center's own app menu.** The page is a new fixed navigation destination asked for only by those two menu items: the tray, which owns the `codex-router://` scheme, still accepts only the usage pages from widget and other external links, so a registered link cannot open it, and the Control Center registers no scheme of its own.
